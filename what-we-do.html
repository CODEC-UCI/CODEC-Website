Our Mission Statement

CODEC stands for Community of Diverse Empowerment in Computing.

CODEC is a community space created to make tech accessible to everyone by sharing resources, supporting one another, and celebrating our diverse experiences in computing.

We strive to build a supportive community where everyone has access to the resources they need to thrive and where respect for one another's backgrounds is key.

What Our Community Offers

  • Professional Workshops

    Explore career paths, get resume help, prepare and practice for interviews, and learn networking tips.

  • Technical Workshops

    Practice technical skills and learn tools and concepts through hands-on activities to build skills for your future in tech.

  • Educational Events

    Learn about computing topics, opportunities, and resources in an approachable setting.

  • Socials

    Meet fellow students, make friends, and find community in a relaxed environment.

  • Mentorship

    Connect with peers and mentors for guidance, encouragement, and shared experience.
